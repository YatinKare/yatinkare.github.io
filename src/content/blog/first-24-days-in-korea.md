Today marks my 24th day studying abroad in Korea, and today I was finally able to rest and share my thoughts about how it's going so far.

## How It's Going

I love Busan so far, I've been to so many places, met so many people, and experienced so many things. I feel like a scientist who's discovered a new frontier and is experiencing a variety of new things.

On the other hand, I am slipping in behaviors that I would've either had in my routine or made time for before coming here. But instead of focusing on the things that I can't do, I'm going to stay in this mindset:

If I believe and tell myself that I can achieve, my imagination is the limit. Do everything with a smile and a passion, letting go of things that hold me back from opportunity.

I had many doubts about what I would be doing and who I would be doing things with while in Korea. But for me, it's been an amazing journey so far.

## What's Next

Going into next week, I'm going to seek the impossible while having as much fun as possible. I will break down my distractions and stand tall in my aspirations.

I have stepped outside of my bubble and I'm noticing how big the world is. I wish to share my experiences with the world as well. I hope to meet many more people, experience many more things, and have a greater impact on the world with my actions.
